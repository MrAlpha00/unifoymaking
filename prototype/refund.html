Refund Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Our Commitment

At UniFoy Making, we are committed to ensuring your satisfaction with our services. We understand that sometimes circumstances change, and you may need to request a refund. This policy outlines our refund terms and procedures.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

You may be eligible for a refund under the following circumstances:

  • The service has not been initiated or started
  • Significant delays due to our team (more than 14 business days without update)
  • Technical issues preventing service delivery
  • Double billing or billing errors

3. Refund Timeline by Service Type

Web Development: Full refund if cancelled before design phase begins. 50% refund if cancelled during design phase. No refund after development has commenced.

Mobile Apps: Full refund if cancelled before discovery phase begins. 50% refund if cancelled during discovery/design. No refund after development has commenced.

UI/UX Design: Full refund if cancelled before design work begins. 50% refund if cancelled after first mockup. No refund after final delivery.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds are not available in the following situations:

  • After significant work has been completed
  • Change of mind after work has commenced
  • Client failure to provide required information
  • Scope changes requested by client
  • Services that have been fully delivered
  • Additional features or revisions outside original scope

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team at support@unifoymaking.com
  • Include your order number and reason for refund request
  • Our team will review your request within 3 business days
  • You will receive a decision notification via email

6. Refund Processing

Once your refund is approved:

  • Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method
  • You will receive a confirmation email when processed
  • Processing time may vary based on your payment provider

7. Partial Refunds

In cases where partial work has been completed, we offer prorated refunds based on the work completed. Our team will provide a detailed breakdown of work completed and associated costs before processing any partial refund.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you're not satisfied with our refund decision, please contact us. We are committed to resolving any disputes fairly and amicably. You can reach our management team at disputes@unifoymaking.com.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Refund Policy, please contact us at support@unifoymaking.com or visit our Contact page.
